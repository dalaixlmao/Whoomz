# v1 — Release 1

**Theme:** conversation-first fitness — one AI coach you can type to or talk to,
with logging that never feels like data entry.

**Design source:** [Whoomz AI board](https://claude.ai/design/p/c3f58faa-3192-49d3-8c58-424425a91168?file=Whoomz+AI.dc.html)

---

## Unified AI conversation (text + voice)

**Status:** in progress
**Why:** users shouldn't manage two assistants — typing and talking are the same coach, same session, same history.

One endpoint (`POST /api/v1/chat`, `mode: text | voice`). Replies stream
sentence-by-sentence behind a blinking caret; coach logs food/workouts via tools
and the transcript shows quiet receipts ("LOGGED · CHICKEN BOWL · 610 KCAL").
Remaining: end-to-end run against production Supabase + AI provider.

## Voice mode

**Status:** in progress
**Why:** hands-free logging mid-workout or mid-meal.

Orb breathes with mic amplitude; on-device STT/TTS for v1 (Deepgram Flux +
ElevenLabs server pipeline deferred — see v1_2). Tap anywhere returns to text.
Remaining: verify on a physical iPhone (simulator has no mic).

## Today screen

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** one glance answers "how am I doing today" — a number, a whisper, a line.

Kcal total, AI daily note, weight + weekly delta, sparkline, workout line.

## Quick log

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** logging a known meal or weigh-in should take under 5 seconds.

Keypad sheet from long-press on any number; MEAL/WEIGHT tabs; recent-food chips;
one gesture to dismiss.

## Progress

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** the trend matters more than the datapoint.

Weight / Calories toggle; no grid, no axes — the line, the dots, one value.
Reachable by typing or saying "progress".

## Auth

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** table stakes — accounts via Supabase, tokens in secure storage, silent refresh.

## Meals — the day, correctable (2a)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** a mislogged AI estimate shouldn't be stuck in your day forever.

Tap the kcal number → today's logs grouped by meal, swipe left to delete.
Dismissed rows leave instantly; totals correct everywhere.

## Macros on Today (2b)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** the coach already estimates protein/carbs/fat on every log — show it.

One quiet caps line under the number: "KCAL TODAY · P 84 · C 210 · F 56".

## Workout history & detail (2c/2d)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** logged workouts deserve more than one line.

Tap the WORKOUT line → history (in-progress pinned with FINISH), tap through to
detail rendering all five tracking types. Say "workouts" in chat to get there.

## Fix it by talking (2e)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** "remove that samosa" should just work.

New `delete_food_log` coach tool; the transcript shows a
"REMOVED · SAMOSA · 180 KCAL" receipt and Today corrects itself.

## Streak on Today (2f)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** consistency is the product.

"· 12 DAYS LOGGED" folded into the date line; an unlogged today doesn't
break the streak.

## Tweaks (2g)

**Status:** shipped
**Why:** the board promised it — accent, orb and bubble style are switchable.

Long-press the date (or say "tweaks"): cycle accent (Electric/Violet/Ember),
orb (filled/ring), bubble (ink/outline); Remember me; sign out.
